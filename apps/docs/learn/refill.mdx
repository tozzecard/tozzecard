Every minute, the API asks one question: **will the card cover what you usually spend until the agent can next sell at a real price?**

## How much you'll need

The forecast is a plain average, no machine learning:

- It looks at your card payments over the last **28 days**, split into **weekdays** and **weekends**, in your timezone.
- Days before your card existed count at the weekly spend you set in Strategy.
- It adds up the expected spending from now until the **next market open after the coming close**, then adds a **20% buffer**.

The card also keeps a **floor of $10**, whatever the forecast says.

## When it sells

| Market | Card | What happens |
|---|---|---|
| Open | Enough | Nothing |
| Open | Short | Waits for the **last 30 minutes before the close**, then sells |
| Open | Below $10 | Sells now |
| Closed | Above $10 | Waits for the open |
| Closed | Below $10 | Sells only what gets the card back to $10, and only if the price is within **1%** of Friday's close. Otherwise it holds |

Each trade is at least **$5**, Binance's minimum.

## What it sells

The stock that is **furthest above its target**. That way a refill is also a rebalance. Ties go to the stock closest to its close price. Paused, limited or unsupported tokens are skipped.

A bStock is sold straight for USD1. An Ondo stock is sold for USDT, then USDT for USD1. The USD1 is sent to your card.

## Safety rules

- **Dry mode** logs what it would do and never trades. Live mode has to be switched on.
- After a refill, it waits **5 minutes** before the next.
- If a swap is still pending after the timeout, its outcome is **unknown**, and no new trade starts for **30 minutes** until someone checks.
- With the market closed, a sale must pass an executable quote, not just the on-chain price.
- A signed-out Agentic Wallet is **blocked**, not retried.

All of these are in [Parameters](/resources/parameters).
