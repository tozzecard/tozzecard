Every decision is saved with its time, action, stock, amount, reason, status and transaction hashes. You see them in **Strategy → Activity**, and developers can read them from [`GET /agent/decisions`](/developers/agent-api).

## Actions

| Action | Means |
|---|---|
| `none` | The card has enough. Nothing to do |
| `refill` | Sell a stock and send USD1 to the card |
| `hold` | The card needs money, but selling now would cost you, or nothing can be sold |
| `rebalance` | Bring the stocks back to your split |

## Statuses

| Status | Means |
|---|---|
| `logged` | A decision with no trade |
| `dry-run` | A trade it would have made, in dry mode |
| `executed` | Traded and sent. The transactions are linked |
| `failed` | Something went wrong. The error is saved |
| `unknown` | A swap was still pending at the timeout. No new trades for 30 minutes |
| `blocked` | The Agentic Wallet needs a sign-in in the Binance App |

## A real example

From the live API on 10 October 2026, in dry mode, with about $11 in the agent wallet and no single position worth $10:

> Need $10.00 but no sellable position is large enough.

Other reasons the agent writes look like *"Selling $12.00 of NVDAB (most overweight) while the market is open: market closes soon, and you usually spend $10.00 until Mon 9:30 AM NY."* The numbers come from your card and your stocks at that moment.

## Preview

[`GET /agent/preview?at=`](/developers/agent-api) runs the same rules for any time you pick, such as Friday 15:40 in New York, and never trades. The app's **Strategy → Preview** tab uses it.
