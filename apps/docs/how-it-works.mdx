## The flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create the card">
    The app makes a wallet key in your browser and locks it with a passkey (WebAuthn PRF). No seed phrase. We never see the key.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect the agent">
    Sign in to your Binance Agentic Wallet and add **only the card address** to its address book in the Binance App.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick a strategy">
    Target weights (for example 70% NVDA, 30% AAPL) and a rough weekly spend.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Spend">
    Scan a merchant QR, confirm with Face ID, pay in USD1 through B402. Binance pays the gas.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The agent refills, while the market is open">
    It forecasts what you will spend until the next open and, before the close, sells whatever stock is overweight and sends the USD1 to your card. One trade tops up the card and rebalances the portfolio.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## The card

The card is a plain wallet (EOA) whose key is encrypted with your passkey. To pay, the passkey unlocks the key,
the key signs an EIP-3009 `transferWithAuthorization` for USD1, and the merchant hands it to B402, which submits
the transaction and pays its gas. The card only signs; it never sends a transaction, so it holds 0 BNB.

The agent can only **fill** the card, never take from it: the Agentic Wallet sends only to addresses in its
address book, and only you can edit that in the Binance App. A send anywhere else is refused by Binance
(error `351703`).

## What Binance and BNB Chain do

| Piece | Role |
| --- | --- |
| BNB Chain (BSC mainnet) | Where the stock tokens, USD1, swaps and payments live |
| Binance Agentic Wallet (`baw`) | The agent's wallet: buys, sells, sends to the card; the address book is the guard |
| Trading API / DEX router | Swaps stock ↔ USDT/USD1 and executable quotes |
| RWA Data API | Market hours, next open/close, prices: decides "sell now or wait" |
| B402 | The card's payment rail, gas paid by Binance |
| BNB | Gas for the agent wallet only |

No custom smart contracts: every contract involved is Binance's, BNB Chain's or the token issuer's.
