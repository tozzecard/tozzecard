Your card's address works from the moment you create it. Activation gives the card its number, expiry and your name, taken from your verified ID.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Tap Activate your card on Home">
    The app opens Didit, our identity check provider.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take a photo of your ID, then a selfie">
    Follow Didit's prompts. When it's done, you're sent back to the app.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for the result">
    Home shows *Verifying your ID* and updates by itself. It can take a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Results

| You see | Means | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| Your card with your name | Approved | Nothing. History shows *ID verified* |
| *Verifying your ID* | Didit is still checking | Wait; the screen updates by itself |
| *Verification failed* | The check didn't pass | Try again with a clear photo of your ID |
| *This ID already has a card* | One person, one card | Sign in to the card you already made |

## What we keep

Didit tells our API the result directly. We keep the name on the card and a fingerprint (a hash) of your document, so the same ID can't activate a second card. The app never sends us your document.

<Note>
The card number and CVV identify the card in the app only. They are not a Visa or Mastercard number, and no shop accepts them. Payments are signed with your passkey.
</Note>
