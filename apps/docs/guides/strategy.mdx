## Connect the agent

The agent works from **your own Binance Agentic Wallet**, inside the Binance App.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign in to the Agentic Wallet">
    The agent's sign-in shows a QR code. Scan it and confirm in the Binance App. A session lasts up to 48 hours; after that, the agent asks you to confirm again.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Lock it to your card">
    In the Binance App, open your wallet settings and add **your card address** to the address book. Add nothing else.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Leave Developer Mode off">
    In Agentic Wallet settings, keep Developer Mode **off**. While it is on, the wallet can make contract calls that skip the address book, so the agent refuses to run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set a daily limit (optional)">
    Also in Agentic Wallet settings. It caps how much the agent can send in a day.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
The address book is what makes this safe: the Agentic Wallet refuses to send anywhere not in it, and the list can only be changed in the Binance App, not by the agent.
</Tip>

## Pick your split

Open **Strategy → Targets**.

1. Use **−** and **+** to move a stock in steps of 5%, or tap the number to type it.
2. The **Allocated** bar shows the total. It must reach exactly **100%**; the app won't let you go over.
3. Set **Weekly spend**: roughly what you spend in a week. The agent uses it until it has learned your real spending.
4. Tap **Save**.

Only the card the agent refills can change the strategy.

## Watch what it does

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Activity** | Every decision the agent made, with its reason |
| **Preview** | Pick a time in New York and see what the agent would do then. Nothing is traded |

On Home, the **Card | Agent** switch shows the agent wallet: the stocks it holds and how they compare to your targets.
